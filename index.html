Which states use Claude.ai more than their population would predict?

The usage per capita index compares a state’s share of all US Claude.ai conversations with its share of US working-age people. An index of 1.0 means the two match exactly — above 1.0 the state is using Claude.ai more than its population alone would predict, below 1.0 less.
